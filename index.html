Why it wins

Built to be verified, not trusted.

01

Real data

Tests on live Kraken and OKX prices, not simulations. Each day's decision is recorded in the morning and scored in the evening (UTC).

02

Auditable

Every decision is logged with a millisecond UTC timestamp, recorded first and then scored against actual market prices that evening. The times are listed on the track record.

03

Built to scale

Risk gates, position sizing and Jito bundle execution are part of the codebase. The pilot is paper trading only: no real capital is deployed.

04

Transparent

Every prediction is logged and scored against actual prices, and results are reported as they happened. No cherry-picking. No black magic.

05

Traceable

Every decision is timestamped and logged, so results can be reviewed after the fact. A research pilot, not investment advice.

06

Extensible

Starting on Solana with SOL/USDC. Possible future work: more assets and strategies, market-making and liquidation detection.
